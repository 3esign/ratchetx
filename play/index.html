THE RULES

Small game.
Everything leaves a trace.

Seal → prices → reveal

Choose a direction, confidence and stake. Your call is sealed as a commitment. Timepin records qualifying Pyth prices for the entry and exit. Once the result is settled, reveal the saved prediction before the chain's deadline.

Which price counts?

MIN-CAPTURE chooses the earliest qualifying captured print, ordered by publish time, posted slot and message hash. The admissible lag is shorter than the target grid. A later displayed price cannot replace the qualifying entry or exit.

When there is no result

Missing eligible prices, equality or the confidence-band rule can void a shot. A closed account alone does not tell us why: the terminal transaction supplies the receipt. Unknown states stay unknown.

Your reveal key and the public proof

The reveal key contains your prediction and salt. Keep it private until reveal; the backup button downloads it to your device. The public game receipt is a different file. A compact archive can omit prices that were present in the live account.
