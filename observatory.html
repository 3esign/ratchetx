What the oracle actually did, measured by someone with money on it.

RatchetX settles real bets off Pyth's sponsored push feeds on Solana. That makes it an unusual kind of consumer — adversarial, continuous, and unable to look away when a feed misbehaves, because a late publish here is not a log line. It is a voided bet and a refunded stake.

Every synthetic benchmark measures with probe traffic a provider can recognise and route to faster hardware. This measurement is real bets that settle — indistinguishable from organic load, and impossible to optimise for without helping every genuine user. It cannot be gamed, and every number below carries its own blind spot.

The stale tail — the half that costs money

A feed's median cadence rarely matters; the tail does. These are the intervals long enough to leave a bet with no price at expiry. Age and gap are 95th-percentile and max seconds; stale windows counts intervals past our own freshness bound; confidence is the feed's own stated uncertainty in basis points. How it is measured

RatchetX is a consumer that settles real bets on these feeds, so it samples them continuously and keeps what it sees.

Solana accountSubscribe captures each sponsored-account transition; one-minute polling is the fallback. Each PriceUpdateV2 is decoded locally and its owner, discriminator, verification level, feed id, age and confidence are checked before a number is kept.

Reproduce any figure from the raw samples it is computed from: /api/game?action=path&feed=SOL&from=<ms>&to=<ms>
